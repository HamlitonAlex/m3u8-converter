After Downloading: TS to MP4

The downloaded file is in TS format. To convert it to MP4 for universal playback, use FFmpeg:

ffmpeg -i "video.ts" -c copy -bsf:a aac_adtstoasc "video.mp4"

This is a lossless conversion — it copies the video stream without re-encoding, so it takes seconds and preserves original quality. Read our complete TS to MP4 guide for details.
