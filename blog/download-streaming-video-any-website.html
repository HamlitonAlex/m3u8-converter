How to Download Streaming Videos from Any Website (2026)

Published: May 2026  |  Reading time: 5 min

The Universal Method: M3U8/HLS Extraction

Over 80% of video streaming sites use HLS (HTTP Live Streaming) to deliver video. This means they all work the same way under the hood — and you can download from any of them using the same technique.

Here's the complete method that works on virtually every streaming site in 2026.

Step 1: Open Developer Tools

Navigate to the page with the video you want. Press F12 (or Ctrl+Shift+I / Cmd+Option+I on Mac) to open Developer Tools.

Click the Network tab. This shows every file the page loads — including video segments.

Step 2: Start Playing and Filter

  1. Hit play on the video — this is essential. The M3U8 file won't appear until the video starts loading.
  2. In the Network filter box, type m3u8
  3. Look for a file ending in .m3u8
  4. If you see multiple, pick the one with a resolution in the name (like 720p or 1080p)

Step 3: Copy the M3U8 Link

Right-click the .m3u8 entry → Copy → Copy link address.

Some sites use time-limited tokens, so the link may expire in minutes. Use it immediately.

If you see #EXT-X-STREAM-INF tags when you open the link, you have a master playlist. See our M3U8 finding guide for handling this.

Step 4: Download or Stream

Paste the M3U8 link into our online player:

  • Click Play to stream the video instantly in your browser
  • Click Download to save the merged video file to your computer

Platform-Specific Tips

  • YouTube — Uses DASH, not HLS. Use yt-dlp instead
  • Twitter/X — Videos are progressive MP4. Right-click save usually works
  • TikTok — Uses a proprietary format. Third-party downloaders required
  • Most Chinese platforms — HLS with geo-restrictions. Our proxy handles many of these
  • News & TV sites — Typically standard HLS, easy to download
